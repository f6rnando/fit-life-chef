Greek Yogurt Parfait

Layers of vanilla yogurt, macerated berries and crunchy granola in a glass. Ten minutes, looks like a café dessert, eats like a proper breakfast.

Ingredients

Metric first, US measures in brackets. For 1 serving.

  • 250g (1 cup) Greek yogurt, 0% fat
  • 1 tsp honey, plus ½ tsp vanilla extract
  • 150g (5.3 oz) mixed berries
  • 1 tsp sugar or honey, for the berries
  • 40g (⅓ cup) granola
  • 1 tbsp chopped pistachios or almonds
  • 2–3 mint leaves (optional)

Method

  1. Toss the berries with the sugar and leave 10 minutes — they release a spoonable juice that becomes your sauce layer.
  2. Whisk the yogurt with honey and vanilla until glossy.
  3. Layer in a glass: yogurt, berries and juice, granola. Repeat once more, finishing with berries.
  4. Top with pistachios and mint. Serve immediately so the granola stays crunchy.

FitLifeChef tip

Build it in a wide glass, not a tall narrow one — you get an even spoonful of every layer instead of mining through yogurt to find the granola.

Swaps

Add 1 tbsp of lemon curd folded through the yogurt and use crushed amaretti instead of half the granola for a dessert version. Protein stays around 30g.

Storage

Leftovers keep in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 4 days unless the FAQ below says otherwise. Reheat until piping hot throughout.

Questions

Can I meal prep parfaits?

Layer yogurt and berries in jars for up to 3 days, but keep granola in a separate small container and add it at serving time.

How do I make it a dessert?

Add 1 tbsp of lemon curd folded through the yogurt and use crushed amaretti instead of half the granola. Protein stays around 30g.
