Chicken Caesar Flatbread Pizza

Crisp flatbread, garlicky yogurt Caesar sauce, chicken and parmesan, finished with crunchy romaine like the salad — in pizza form, in 20 minutes.

Ingredients

Metric first, US measures in brackets. For 2 servings.

  • 2 large flatbreads or naan, 200g (7 oz) total
  • 250g (8.8 oz) cooked chicken, sliced
  • 150g (5.3 oz) Greek yogurt, 0% fat
  • 1 garlic clove, grated, plus 1 tsp mustard and a squeeze of lemon
  • 80g (2.8 oz) mozzarella, grated
  • 30g (1 oz) parmesan, shaved
  • 100g (3.5 oz) romaine, shredded
  • Black pepper and a pinch of chilli flakes

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 220°C (425°F) with a tray inside — a hot tray is your crispy base guarantee.
  2. Stir the yogurt with garlic, mustard, lemon and half the parmesan into a Caesar sauce. Season boldly.
  3. Spread sauce over the flatbreads, top with mozzarella and chicken.
  4. Bake on the hot tray for 8–10 minutes until bubbling and golden at the edges.
  5. Finish with romaine, the rest of the parmesan, black pepper and chilli flakes. Slice and eat hot.

FitLifeChef tip

Add the romaine after baking, never before — warm wilted lettuce is a tragedy, cold crunchy lettuce on hot pizza is the whole idea.

Swaps

Use a gluten-free flatbread or a large flour tortilla — tortillas crisp even faster, so check at 6 minutes. Slice raw breast very thin and sear it 4 minutes first if you have no cooked chicken.

Storage

Leftovers keep in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 4 days unless the FAQ below says otherwise. Reheat until piping hot throughout.

Questions

Can I use raw chicken?

Slice 250g (8.8 oz) of breast very thin and sear it 4 minutes first. Thick raw chicken won't cook through in the pizza's 10 minutes.

Gluten-free option?

Use a gluten-free flatbread or a large flour tortilla — tortillas crisp even faster, so check at 6 minutes.
