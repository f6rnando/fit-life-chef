Baked Oat Bowl

A single-serve baked oat bowl with a cake-like middle, made with Greek yogurt and protein powder so breakfast actually keeps you full.

Ingredients

Metric first, US measures in brackets. For 1 serving.

  • 50g (½ cup) rolled oats
  • 30g (1 scoop) vanilla protein powder
  • 100g (3.5 oz) Greek yogurt, 0% fat
  • 1 egg
  • 80ml (⅓ cup) milk of choice
  • ½ tsp baking powder
  • 80g (2.8 oz) mixed berries, fresh or frozen
  • 1 tsp honey, to serve

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 180°C (350°F). Lightly oil a small ovenproof bowl or ramekin, about 400ml (14 oz).
  2. Blend the oats to a rough flour, then whisk with the protein powder and baking powder.
  3. Whisk in the yogurt, egg and milk until you have a thick batter. Fold in half the berries.
  4. Pour into the bowl, scatter the rest of the berries on top, and bake 20–22 minutes until risen and just set in the centre.
  5. Rest 2 minutes, drizzle with honey, and eat it warm with a spoon.

FitLifeChef tip

Blend the batter completely smooth if you want a true cake texture; leave the oats whole for a more traditional baked-oats chew.

Swaps

Swap the berries for diced apple or pear, and the vanilla protein powder for chocolate. See the FAQ for a no-protein-powder version.

Storage

Leftovers keep in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 4 days unless the FAQ below says otherwise. Reheat until piping hot throughout.

Questions

Can I make this ahead?

Yes — bake a batch of 4 in ramekins, chill, and reheat 60–90 seconds in the microwave. They keep 3 days in the fridge.

No protein powder — what instead?

Swap it for 30g (¼ cup) extra oat flour plus 50g (1.8 oz) more Greek yogurt. Protein lands nearer 35g — still a solid breakfast.
