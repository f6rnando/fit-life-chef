Protein Ramen with Soft Eggs and Chicken

A weeknight ramen that earns its name: chicken, two soft eggs and noodles in a ginger-miso broth that tastes like it simmered for hours. It didn't.

Ingredients

Metric first, US measures in brackets. For 2 servings.

  • 2 chicken breasts, 340g (12 oz) total
  • 4 eggs
  • 120g (4.2 oz) ramen noodles, dry
  • 1 litre (4¼ cups) chicken stock
  • 2 tbsp white miso paste
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce, plus 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 2 garlic cloves, grated
  • 150g (5.3 oz) baby spinach or pak choi
  • 2 spring onions and chilli oil, to finish

Method

  1. Lower the eggs into boiling water for exactly 6½ minutes, then straight into cold water. Peel carefully — they're soft inside.
  2. Simmer the chicken in the stock for 10 minutes. Lift it out, slice it, and keep the stock as your broth base.
  3. Whisk miso, soy, ginger and garlic into the hot stock. Taste — it should be savoury first, salty second.
  4. Cook the noodles in the broth for 3 minutes, adding the greens for the last minute.
  5. Divide between bowls, top with chicken, halved soft eggs, spring onions and chilli oil.

FitLifeChef tip

Whisk the miso with a ladle of hot broth in a mug first. Dumping the paste straight in leaves salty lumps that never dissolve.

Swaps

Use 250g (8.8 oz) leftover roast chicken in place of the simmered chicken — skip step 2 and pour the hot broth over to warm it through.

Storage

Leftovers keep in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 4 days unless the FAQ below says otherwise. Reheat until piping hot throughout.

Questions

Can I use leftover roast chicken?

Perfect for it. Skip step 2, add 250g (8.8 oz) shredded chicken to the bowls and pour the hot broth over to warm it through.

How do I get the eggs to peel cleanly?

Week-old eggs peel easier than very fresh ones, and the cold-water shock right after cooking is non-negotiable.
