Steak and Crispy Potato Power Bowl

Sliced steak over shatter-crisp potatoes with greens, pickled onion and a yogurt chimichurri. Steak night, rebuilt as a bowl you'll crave weekly.

Ingredients

Metric first, US measures in brackets. For 2 servings.

  • 2 sirloin steaks, 400g (14 oz) total
  • 400g (14 oz) baby potatoes, halved
  • 1 red onion, thinly sliced, plus 2 tbsp vinegar for quick pickling
  • 100g (3.5 oz) rocket or spinach
  • 100g (3.5 oz) Greek yogurt, 0% fat
  • 1 tbsp chimichurri or finely chopped parsley, garlic and chilli
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Flaky salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Toss the onion with vinegar and a pinch of salt and sugar; set aside to quick-pickle while everything cooks.
  2. Parboil the potatoes 6 minutes, drain, rough up the edges, then roast or air fry at 200°C (400°F) with the oil and salt for 18–20 minutes until crisp.
  3. Sear the steaks in a smoking-hot pan, 3 minutes per side for medium-rare. Rest 5 minutes — non-negotiable — then slice against the grain.
  4. Stir the chimichurri through the yogurt for the sauce.
  5. Bowl it up: potatoes, greens, steak, pickled onion and a generous spoon of chimichurri yogurt.

FitLifeChef tip

Slice against the grain and at an angle. The same steak, sliced the wrong way, chews twice as tough — the knife work is the tenderness.

Swaps

Sirloin and bavette/flank both work. Bavette is cheaper and deeply beefy — just don't take it past medium or it tightens up. Swap potatoes for sweet potato wedges or crispy chickpeas (200g / 7 oz, roasted with paprika).

Storage

Leftovers keep in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 4 days unless the FAQ below says otherwise. Reheat until piping hot throughout.

Questions

Which steak cut is best value?

Sirloin and bavette/flank both work. Bavette is cheaper and deeply beefy — just don't take it past medium or it tightens up.

Can I swap the potatoes?

Sweet potato wedges or crispy chickpeas (200g / 7 oz, roasted with paprika) both keep the bowl's crunch-and-soft contrast.
