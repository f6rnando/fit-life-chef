Air Fryer Chicken Meal Prep Bowl

Air fryer chicken with crisp edges, fluffy rice and garlicky green beans — four lunches in under 30 minutes, with a yogurt ranch to keep them interesting.

Ingredients

Metric first, US measures in brackets. For 4 servings.

  • 600g (1 lb 5 oz) chicken breast, cubed
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika, 1 tsp garlic powder, 1 tsp onion powder
  • 300g (1½ cups) long-grain rice, uncooked
  • 400g (14 oz) green beans, trimmed
  • 2 garlic cloves, grated
  • 200g (7 oz) Greek yogurt, 0% fat
  • 1 tsp dried dill and ½ tsp mustard, for the ranch
  • Lemon wedges, to serve

Method

  1. Toss the chicken with the spices, 1 tbsp oil and salt until every piece is coated.
  2. Air fry at 200°C (400°F) for 14–16 minutes, shaking the basket halfway, until crisp-edged and 75°C (167°F) inside.
  3. Meanwhile cook the rice and steam or boil the green beans for 4 minutes, then toss them with garlic and a pinch of salt.
  4. Stir the yogurt with dill, mustard, a squeeze of lemon and salt into a ranch sauce.
  5. Divide rice, beans and chicken between 4 containers. Sauce in a separate pot, lemon wedge on top.

FitLifeChef tip

Cube the chicken evenly at about 3cm (1¼ in) — mixed sizes are the only way air fryer chicken ends up half dry, half underdone.

Swaps

No air fryer? Roast the chicken on a tray at 220°C (425°F) for 16–18 minutes. Swap green beans for broccoli florets if you prefer.

Storage

Leftovers keep in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 4 days unless the FAQ below says otherwise. Reheat until piping hot throughout.

Questions

No air fryer?

Roast the chicken on a tray at 220°C (425°F) for 16–18 minutes. You lose a little crispness but none of the flavour.

How long do the bowls keep?

4 days in the fridge. Reheat the chicken and rice together for 2½ minutes; add the cold ranch after.
