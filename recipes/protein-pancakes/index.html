Protein Pancakes

A tall stack of fluffy pancakes made from oats, cottage cheese and eggs — blended in one jug, with berries and yogurt instead of a sugar crash.

Ingredients

Metric first, US measures in brackets. For 1 serving.

  • 80g (¾ cup) rolled oats
  • 150g (5.3 oz) cottage cheese
  • 2 eggs
  • 30g (1 scoop) vanilla protein powder
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • ½ tsp cinnamon
  • 100g (3.5 oz) berries, to serve
  • 100g (3.5 oz) Greek yogurt and 1 tsp honey, to serve

Method

  1. Blend everything except the toppings until smooth. Rest the batter 5 minutes — it thickens as the oats hydrate, and that rest is the fluff.
  2. Heat a non-stick pan on medium-low with a wipe of oil. Low and slow is the rule for thick pancakes.
  3. Add 3 tbsp batter per pancake. When bubbles hold their shape on top, about 2 minutes, flip and cook 1 minute more.
  4. Stack, spoon over the yogurt, berries and honey.
  5. Any leftovers freeze brilliantly — reheat from frozen in the toaster.

FitLifeChef tip

If the first pancake spreads too thin, whisk 1 extra tbsp of oats into the batter. Protein powders vary in absorbency, so judge by thickness, not the clock.

Swaps

Skip the protein powder and add 20g (3 tbsp) more oats — the stack lands around 51g protein. Swap berries for sliced banana.

Storage

Leftovers keep in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 4 days unless the FAQ below says otherwise. Reheat until piping hot throughout.

Questions

Can I skip the protein powder?

Yes — add 20g (3 tbsp) more oats. The stack lands around 51g protein, still well over double a standard pancake breakfast.

Why are mine dense?

The pan was too hot or the batter wasn't rested. Protein batter needs gentle heat and that 5-minute rest to set its crumb.
