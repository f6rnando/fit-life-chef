Hot Honey Salmon Rice Bowl

Crispy-edged salmon glazed with hot honey over warm rice, with cucumber, avocado and a lime-yogurt drizzle. Sweet heat, serious protein, zero fuss.

Ingredients

Metric first, US measures in brackets. For 2 servings.

  • 2 salmon fillets, 300g (10.5 oz) total, skin on
  • 200g (1 cup) jasmine rice, uncooked
  • 30ml (2 tbsp) honey
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 100g (3.5 oz) cucumber, ribboned
  • 1 avocado, 150g (5.3 oz), sliced
  • 100g (3.5 oz) Greek yogurt, 0% fat
  • Juice of 1 lime, plus sesame seeds to finish

Method

  1. Cook the rice in 300ml (1¼ cups) water, cover and rest 10 minutes.
  2. Stir the honey, chilli flakes and soy sauce together. Pat the salmon dry and season with salt.
  3. Sear salmon skin-side down in a hot non-stick pan for 5 minutes, flip, and brush generously with the hot honey glaze. Cook 2 more minutes until sticky and just cooked through.
  4. Stir the lime juice into the yogurt with a pinch of salt.
  5. Bowl it up: rice, salmon, cucumber and avocado, then the lime yogurt and sesame seeds.

FitLifeChef tip

Keep the salmon skin on and start it in a cold-ish pan pressed flat for 10 seconds — that's how you get shatter-crisp skin instead of it curling.

Swaps

Trout fillets work in place of salmon; use the same glaze and timing. For a lighter bowl, swap half the rice for shredded cabbage.

Storage

Leftovers keep in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 4 days unless the FAQ below says otherwise. Reheat until piping hot throughout.

Questions

Can I use frozen salmon?

Yes — thaw it fully in the fridge and pat it very dry before searing, or the glaze will slide off instead of sticking.

How spicy is the hot honey?

Mild-medium at 1 tsp chilli flakes. Halve it for gentle warmth, or add a sliced fresh chilli on top if you like real heat.
