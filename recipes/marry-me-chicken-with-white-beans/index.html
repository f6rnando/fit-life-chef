Marry Me Chicken with White Beans

The viral creamy sun-dried tomato chicken, made weeknight-light with Greek yogurt and bulked out with white beans so one pan feeds four, properly.

Ingredients

Metric first, US measures in brackets. For 4 servings.

  • 4 chicken breasts, 700g (1 lb 9 oz) total
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 100g (3.5 oz) sun-dried tomatoes in oil, drained and sliced
  • 1 tsp Italian seasoning
  • 250ml (1 cup) chicken stock
  • 150g (5.3 oz) Greek yogurt, 0% fat
  • 400g (14 oz) tinned cannellini beans, drained
  • 30g (1 oz) parmesan, finely grated
  • Handful of basil leaves, torn

Method

  1. Season the chicken and sear in 1 tbsp of the tomato oil, 5 minutes per side, until golden. Set aside.
  2. In the same pan, soften the garlic and sun-dried tomatoes for 1 minute with the Italian seasoning.
  3. Pour in the stock and simmer 3 minutes, scraping up the browned bits — that's the flavour.
  4. Lower the heat, stir in the yogurt and parmesan until glossy, then add the beans.
  5. Return the chicken, cover, and simmer 6–8 minutes until cooked through. Finish with basil.

FitLifeChef tip

Take the pan off the heat before stirring in the yogurt and it will never split — boiling is what makes yogurt sauces grainy.

Swaps

Boneless chicken thighs work — same method, add 3–4 minutes to the simmer. Serve with bread, mashed potato or pasta, as in the FAQ.

Storage

Leftovers keep in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 4 days unless the FAQ below says otherwise. Reheat until piping hot throughout.

Questions

What do I serve it with?

Crusty bread, mashed potato or 200g (7 oz) of cooked pasta stirred straight through the sauce all work. The beans already make it filling on its own.

Can I use chicken thighs?

Yes, boneless thighs are great here — same method, add 3–4 minutes to the simmer. Protein per serving lands about 5g lower.
