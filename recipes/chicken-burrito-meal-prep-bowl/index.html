Chicken Burrito Meal Prep Bowl

Four grab-and-go burrito bowls: chipotle chicken, cilantro-lime rice, black beans, corn and peppers, with a yogurt-lime sauce packed on the side.

Ingredients

Metric first, US measures in brackets. For 4 servings.

  • 600g (1 lb 5 oz) chicken breast
  • 2 tbsp chipotle paste
  • 300g (1½ cups) long-grain rice, uncooked
  • 400g (14 oz) tinned black beans, drained
  • 200g (7 oz) sweetcorn
  • 2 bell peppers, 300g (10.5 oz), sliced
  • 1 red onion, sliced
  • 150g (5.3 oz) Greek yogurt, 0% fat, plus lime juice and coriander
  • 1 lime, cut into wedges

Method

  1. Coat the chicken in chipotle paste, 1 tbsp oil and salt. Rest while the oven heats to 200°C (400°F).
  2. Cook the rice, then stir through lime zest, juice and chopped coriander stems.
  3. Roast the chicken, peppers and onion on one tray for 18–20 minutes until the chicken hits 75°C (167°F). Rest, then slice.
  4. Warm the beans and corn together with a pinch of cumin and salt.
  5. Divide rice, beans, veg and chicken between 4 containers. Pack the yogurt-lime sauce separately and add after reheating.

FitLifeChef tip

Keep the sauce and any salad in a separate small pot — reheating the bowl while the sauce stays cold is what makes day-4 meal prep still taste fresh.

Swaps

Chicken thighs work in place of breast — add a few minutes to the roast. Swap black beans for kidney beans if you prefer.

Storage

Leftovers keep in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 4 days unless the FAQ below says otherwise. Reheat until piping hot throughout.

Questions

How long do these keep?

4 days in the fridge at or below 4°C (39°F). Freeze the chicken-and-rice portion for up to 3 months; add sauce fresh.

How do I reheat without drying the chicken?

Sprinkle 1 tbsp water over the bowl, cover loosely, and microwave 2½–3 minutes, stirring the rice halfway.
