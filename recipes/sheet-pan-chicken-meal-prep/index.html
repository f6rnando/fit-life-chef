Sheet Pan Chicken Meal Prep

One tray, four lunches: chicken thighs, crispy potatoes and broccoli in a garlic-herb rub. The easiest meal prep of the week.

By FitLifeChef · Published Saturday, November 28, 2026 · Nutrition calculated per serving from the ingredients below — method and source under the recipe card.

Ingredients

Metric first, US measures in brackets. For 4 servings.

  • 700g (1 lb 9 oz) boneless chicken thighs
  • 600g (1 lb 5 oz) baby potatoes, halved
  • 400g (14 oz) broccoli florets
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tsp garlic powder, 2 tsp Italian seasoning, 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 lemon, half juiced, half in wedges
  • 40g (1.4 oz) parmesan, grated (optional)
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 210°C (410°F). Toss the potatoes with half the oil and spices and spread on a large tray, cut-side down.
  2. Roast the potatoes alone for 12 minutes — they need a head start to get crisp.
  3. Add the chicken and broccoli, toss with the remaining oil and spices, and spread everything in one layer.
  4. Roast 16–18 minutes more until the chicken is cooked through and the potato edges are golden.
  5. Squeeze over the lemon juice, shower with parmesan, and divide between 4 containers with a lemon wedge each.

FitLifeChef tip

One layer, no touching. The moment ingredients pile up they steam instead of roast, and you lose every crispy edge you came for.

Swaps

Chicken breast works if cut into thick strips so it finishes in the same time as the veg. Swap broccoli for green beans.

Storage

Leftovers keep in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 4 days unless the FAQ below says otherwise. Reheat until piping hot throughout.

Questions

Breast instead of thighs?

Works — cut breasts into thick strips so they finish in the same time as the veg, and check they don't overcook. Thighs forgive reheating better.

How long does it keep?

4 days refrigerated. Reheat in a 180°C (350°F) oven for 10 minutes to bring the crisp back; the microwave softens it.
