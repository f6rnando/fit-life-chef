Tinned Fish Pasta with Lemon and Herbs

Sardines and tuna are the most underrated protein in the cupboard. Fifteen minutes, one pan of pasta, lemon, garlic, chilli and herbs — dinner done.

Ingredients

Metric first, US measures in brackets. For 2 servings.

  • 200g (7 oz) spaghetti or linguine
  • 2 tins sardines in olive oil, 240g (8.5 oz) total, drained
  • 1 tin tuna, 145g (5.1 oz), drained
  • 3 garlic cloves, sliced, plus ½ tsp chilli flakes
  • Zest and juice of 1 lemon
  • 100g (3.5 oz) rocket
  • Big handful of parsley, chopped
  • 30g (1 oz) parmesan, to serve

Method

  1. Cook the pasta in well-salted water. Reserve 250ml (1 cup) of pasta water before draining.
  2. Warm the sardine oil (or 2 tbsp olive oil) in a wide pan and gently fry the garlic and chilli for 1 minute — golden, not brown.
  3. Add the sardines and tuna in big chunks and warm through for 2 minutes. Break them up only a little; texture is the point.
  4. Add the drained pasta with splashes of pasta water, the lemon zest and juice, and toss until glossy.
  5. Fold through the rocket and parsley off the heat. Serve with parmesan and more lemon at the table.

FitLifeChef tip

Warm the fish, don't fry it. Two gentle minutes keeps sardines silky; hard frying makes them dry and overpowering.

Swaps

Use 2 tins of tuna (290g / 10 oz total) if you have no sardines. Add a drained tin of white beans as well and you'll keep the protein right where it was.

Storage

Leftovers keep in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 4 days unless the FAQ below says otherwise. Reheat until piping hot throughout.

Questions

I only have tuna — fine?

Yes, use 2 tins (290g / 10 oz total). Add a drained tin of white beans as well and you'll keep the protein right where it was.

Does it reheat?

Pasta with fish is best fresh, but leftovers make an excellent cold pasta salad the next day with extra lemon and olive oil.
