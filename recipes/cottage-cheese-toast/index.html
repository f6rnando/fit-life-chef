Cottage Cheese Toast

Whipped cottage cheese on thick sourdough with jammy tomatoes, basil and chilli honey. The snack that eats like a café lunch.

Ingredients

Metric first, US measures in brackets. For 1 serving.

  • 1 thick slice sourdough, 60g (2.1 oz)
  • 150g (5.3 oz) cottage cheese
  • 100g (3.5 oz) cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1 tsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp honey plus a pinch of chilli flakes
  • 4–5 basil leaves
  • Flaky salt and black pepper
  • Squeeze of lemon

Method

  1. Whip the cottage cheese with the lemon juice and a pinch of salt until fluffy — a fork works, a small blender is faster.
  2. Toast the sourdough until deeply golden and crisp at the edges.
  3. Tumble the tomatoes in the olive oil and salt; leave 5 minutes so they get juicy.
  4. Pile the whipped cottage cheese onto the toast, spoon the tomatoes and their juices over the top.
  5. Finish with basil, chilli honey and black pepper. Eat immediately, over the plate — it drips, gloriously.

FitLifeChef tip

Salting the tomatoes 5 minutes ahead is the difference between a watery toast and a jammy one. Don't skip the wait.

Swaps

Swap tomatoes for 80g (2.8 oz) sliced strawberries or peach, keep the chilli honey, and add a crack of black pepper. Add a sliced boiled egg for +6g protein.

Storage

Leftovers keep in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 4 days unless the FAQ below says otherwise. Reheat until piping hot throughout.

Questions

Sweet version?

Swap tomatoes for 80g (2.8 oz) sliced strawberries or peach, keep the chilli honey, and add a crack of black pepper. Sounds odd, tastes incredible.

How do I boost the protein further?

Add a sliced boiled egg on top (+6g) or use two slices of toast with 200g (7 oz) of cottage cheese (+10g).
