Grilled Chicken Power Bowl

Smoky grilled chicken over quinoa with roasted sweet potato, greens and a tahini-lemon drizzle. The template bowl you'll make on repeat.

Ingredients

Metric first, US measures in brackets. For 2 servings.

  • 2 chicken breasts, 340g (12 oz) total
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika and ½ tsp garlic powder
  • 150g (¾ cup) quinoa, uncooked
  • 1 sweet potato, 250g (8.8 oz), cubed
  • 100g (3.5 oz) kale or spinach
  • 30g (2 tbsp) tahini
  • Juice of 1 lemon, plus 2–3 tbsp water
  • 40g (1.4 oz) feta, crumbled

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 200°C (400°F). Roast the sweet potato in 1 tsp oil and salt for 20 minutes — start this first.
  2. Rub the chicken with paprika, garlic powder, salt and 1 tsp oil. Grill or sear 6–7 minutes per side; rest 5 minutes, then slice.
  3. Simmer the quinoa in 300ml (1¼ cups) water for 14 minutes, then steam off the heat for 5.
  4. Whisk tahini, lemon juice, water and salt into a pourable drizzle.
  5. Bowl it up: quinoa, greens, sweet potato and chicken, then tahini drizzle and feta.

FitLifeChef tip

Resting the chicken for a full 5 minutes before slicing is worth 10g of juices staying in the meat instead of on the board.

Swaps

Couscous (10 minutes, even faster), brown rice or a 250g (8.8 oz) pouch of cooked grains all work in place of quinoa, with the same toppings.

Storage

Leftovers keep in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 4 days unless the FAQ below says otherwise. Reheat until piping hot throughout.

Questions

No quinoa — what instead?

Couscous (10 minutes, even faster), brown rice or a 250g (8.8 oz) pouch of cooked grains all work with the same toppings.

How do I stop kale being tough?

Massage it for 30 seconds with a pinch of salt and a squeeze of lemon — it softens, darkens and tastes far less bitter.
