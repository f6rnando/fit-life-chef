Protein Ice Cream

Thick, scoopable chocolate protein ice cream blended from cottage cheese and frozen banana — no ice-cream maker, no chalky aftertaste.

Ingredients

Metric first, US measures in brackets. For 2 servings.

  • 300g (10.5 oz) cottage cheese
  • 2 frozen bananas, 240g (8.5 oz), in chunks
  • 60g (2 scoops) chocolate protein powder
  • 20g (3 tbsp) cocoa powder
  • 30ml (2 tbsp) honey or maple syrup
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • Pinch of sea salt

Method

  1. Blend the cottage cheese completely smooth first — 60 seconds, scraping down once. This step is the whole trick.
  2. Add the frozen banana, protein powder, cocoa, honey, vanilla and salt. Blend, tamping down, until it looks like soft-serve.
  3. Eat it now as soft-serve, or scrape into a tub and freeze 1½–2 hours for firm scoops.
  4. If frozen solid, rest on the counter 10 minutes before scooping.

FitLifeChef tip

Freeze it in a shallow tub, not a deep one — it thaws evenly and you skip the rock-hard middle problem entirely.

Swaps

Swap the cocoa for 100g (3.5 oz) frozen strawberries for strawberry, or add 1 tsp instant coffee for mocha. Keep the base the same.

Storage

Leftovers keep in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 4 days unless the FAQ below says otherwise. Reheat until piping hot throughout.

Questions

Does it taste like cottage cheese?

No. Once blended smooth and frozen with cocoa and banana, it reads as chocolate ice cream. The cottage cheese is there for body and protein only.

Can I make other flavours?

Swap the cocoa for 100g (3.5 oz) frozen strawberries for strawberry, or add 1 tsp instant coffee for mocha. Keep the base the same.
