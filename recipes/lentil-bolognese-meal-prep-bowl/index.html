Lentil Bolognese Meal Prep Bowl

A rich, slow-tasting lentil bolognese over pasta with parmesan and a spoon of Greek yogurt — the plant-based meal prep that still clears 25g protein per serving, and in this version lands at 40g.

Ingredients

Metric first, US measures in brackets. For 4 servings.

  • 300g (1½ cups) dried red or green lentils, rinsed
  • 1 onion, 2 carrots and 2 celery sticks, finely diced (300g / 10.5 oz total)
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 400g (14 oz) tinned chopped tomatoes, plus 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 750ml (3 cups) vegetable stock
  • 320g (11.3 oz) penne or fusilli
  • 40g (1.4 oz) parmesan, grated
  • 200g (7 oz) Greek yogurt, 0% fat, to serve

Method

  1. Soften the onion, carrot and celery in 1 tbsp olive oil for 8 minutes until sweet and golden at the edges. Add the garlic for 1 minute.
  2. Stir in the tomato paste, then the lentils, tomatoes and stock. Simmer 22–25 minutes until the lentils are tender and the sauce is thick.
  3. Meanwhile cook the pasta until just shy of done — it finishes in the containers' residual heat when reheated.
  4. Season the bolognese boldly: salt, pepper, and a pinch of sugar if the tomatoes are sharp.
  5. Divide pasta and sauce between 4 containers. Top each with parmesan and a spoon of yogurt after reheating.

FitLifeChef tip

Undercook the pasta by 2 minutes for meal prep. It absorbs sauce in the fridge and reheats to perfect instead of bloated.

Swaps

Stir 200g (7 oz) of blended cottage cheese through the hot sauce (+22g across the batch) or serve with a boiled egg on top (+6g per bowl).

Storage

Leftovers keep in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 4 days unless the FAQ below says otherwise. Reheat until piping hot throughout.

Questions

How do I push the protein higher?

Stir 200g (7 oz) of blended cottage cheese through the hot sauce (+22g across the batch) or serve with a boiled egg on top (+6g per bowl).

How long does it keep?

5 days in the fridge — lentil sauces genuinely improve by day two. It also freezes for 3 months without losing texture.
