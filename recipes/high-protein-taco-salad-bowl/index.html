High-Protein Taco Salad Bowl

Seasoned lean beef, black beans and crunchy romaine under a Greek yogurt taco sauce, with crushed tortilla chips for the crunch you actually want.

Ingredients

Metric first, US measures in brackets. For 2 servings.

  • 300g (10.5 oz) lean beef mince, 5% fat
  • 1 tbsp taco seasoning
  • 200g (7 oz) tinned black beans, drained
  • 1 romaine lettuce, 200g (7 oz), shredded
  • 150g (5.3 oz) cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 80g (2.8 oz) cheddar, grated
  • 100g (3.5 oz) Greek yogurt, 0% fat, plus 1 tsp taco seasoning and lime juice
  • 30g (1 oz) tortilla chips, crushed
  • 1 avocado, sliced (optional)

Method

  1. Brown the mince in a dry hot pan, breaking it up, then stir in the taco seasoning and 3 tbsp water. Simmer 2 minutes.
  2. Warm the beans in the same pan once the beef is out, with a pinch of salt.
  3. Stir the yogurt, extra seasoning and lime juice into a pourable sauce.
  4. Build the bowls: lettuce, beans, beef, tomatoes, cheddar and avocado.
  5. Drizzle with sauce and finish with crushed chips right before eating so they stay crunchy.

FitLifeChef tip

Add the chips at the table, never before — 30g (1 oz) across two bowls gives full crunch for very few calories if they stay crisp.

Swaps

Turkey mince, shredded chicken or 300g (10.5 oz) of crumbled firm tofu with the same seasoning all land within a few grams of protein.

Storage

Leftovers keep in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 4 days unless the FAQ below says otherwise. Reheat until piping hot throughout.

Questions

Can I meal prep this?

Prep the beef, beans and sauce for up to 4 days. Store the lettuce, tomatoes and chips separately and assemble in 2 minutes.

What protein swaps work?

Turkey mince, shredded chicken or 300g (10.5 oz) of crumbled firm tofu with the same seasoning all land within a few grams of protein.
