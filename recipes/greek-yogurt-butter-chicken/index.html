Greek Yogurt Butter Chicken

All the warmth of butter chicken, lightened with a Greek yogurt marinade and sauce. The yogurt does double duty — tenderiser first, creaminess second.

Ingredients

Metric first, US measures in brackets. For 4 servings.

  • 700g (1 lb 9 oz) chicken breast, cubed
  • 300g (10.5 oz) Greek yogurt, 0% fat, divided
  • 2 tbsp tandoori or tikka spice paste
  • 400g (14 oz) tinned chopped tomatoes
  • 20g (1½ tbsp) butter
  • 3 garlic cloves and 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 200g (1 cup) basmati rice, uncooked, to serve
  • Coriander leaves, to finish

Method

  1. Marinate the chicken in half the yogurt, the spice paste and salt for at least 15 minutes (overnight is better).
  2. Sear the chicken in a hot pan until charred at the edges, about 6 minutes. It finishes in the sauce, so don't cook it through yet.
  3. Melt the butter, fry the garlic and ginger 1 minute, add tomatoes and garam masala, and simmer 8 minutes until thick.
  4. Blend the sauce smooth, return to the pan, and stir in the remaining yogurt off the heat.
  5. Add the chicken back, simmer gently 5 minutes, and serve over basmati with coriander.

FitLifeChef tip

Yogurt marinade + a ripping hot pan gives you tandoori-style charred edges without a grill — don't crowd the pan or the chicken steams.

Swaps

Boneless thighs work in place of breast. For mild, use 1 tbsp paste and add 1 tsp honey to the sauce; for hot, add a sliced green chilli with the garlic.

Storage

Leftovers keep in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 4 days unless the FAQ below says otherwise. Reheat until piping hot throughout.

Questions

Is it very spicy?

Tikka paste is warm rather than hot. For mild, use 1 tbsp paste and add 1 tsp honey to the sauce; for hot, add a sliced green chilli with the garlic.

Can I freeze it?

Yes, up to 3 months. Yogurt sauces can look slightly split after thawing — whisk in 2 tbsp fresh yogurt while reheating and it comes back together.
