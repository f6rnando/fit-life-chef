Cottage Cheese Alfredo Pasta

A proper creamy Alfredo built on blended cottage cheese instead of a pot of cream — with chicken through it so it eats like a real dinner, not a side.

Ingredients

Metric first, US measures in brackets. For 2 servings.

  • 200g (7 oz) fettuccine or penne
  • 300g (10.5 oz) cottage cheese
  • 2 chicken breasts, 340g (12 oz) total
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 40g (1.4 oz) parmesan, grated, plus more to serve
  • 120ml (½ cup) milk
  • ½ tsp nutmeg (optional)
  • 150g (5.3 oz) baby spinach
  • Black pepper, plenty

Method

  1. Cook the pasta in well-salted water. Reserve 250ml (1 cup) of pasta water before draining — the sauce needs it.
  2. Sear the seasoned chicken 6 minutes per side until cooked through. Rest, then slice.
  3. Blend the cottage cheese, milk, garlic and parmesan until completely smooth and silky.
  4. Pour the sauce into the pasta pot on the lowest heat, add the pasta and spinach, and toss with splashes of pasta water until glossy. Never let it boil.
  5. Serve topped with the sliced chicken, extra parmesan and lots of black pepper.

FitLifeChef tip

Blend the cottage cheese for a full 90 seconds. Stop at 30 and it's grainy; push to 90 and nobody at the table will ever guess the swap.

Swaps

Skip the chicken and add 200g (7 oz) of mushrooms fried hard in the pasta pan for a vegetarian version. Protein lands around 41g per serving.

Storage

Leftovers keep in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 4 days unless the FAQ below says otherwise. Reheat until piping hot throughout.

Questions

Why did my sauce go thin?

It was probably too hot. Cottage cheese sauces loosen when boiled — keep it at a bare simmer and use pasta water to adjust, a splash at a time.

Can I make it vegetarian?

Skip the chicken and add 200g (7 oz) of mushrooms fried hard in the pasta pan. Protein lands around 41g per serving.
