Greek Yogurt Berry Bowl

Thick Greek yogurt whipped with vanilla and honey, piled with berries, granola crunch and chia. Five minutes, no cooking, 31g of protein.

Ingredients

Metric first, US measures in brackets. For 1 serving.

  • 250g (1 cup) Greek yogurt, 0% fat
  • 15g (½ scoop) vanilla protein powder (optional, adds 12g protein)
  • 1 tsp honey
  • ½ tsp vanilla extract
  • 150g (5.3 oz) mixed berries
  • 30g (⅓ cup) granola
  • 1 tbsp chia seeds
  • A few mint leaves (optional)

Method

  1. Whisk the yogurt with the protein powder, honey and vanilla until thick and glossy — whisking stops the powder tasting grainy.
  2. Spoon into a wide bowl and smooth the top with the back of the spoon.
  3. Pile on the berries, granola and chia seeds in sections so every spoonful gets a bit of everything.
  4. Eat straight away while the granola is still crunchy.

FitLifeChef tip

A wide, shallow bowl beats a deep one here — more surface area means more topping in every bite, which is the entire point of a yogurt bowl.

Swaps

Use frozen berries, microwaved 40 seconds, in place of fresh. Swap granola for chopped nuts if you prefer a lower-sugar crunch.

Storage

Leftovers keep in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 4 days unless the FAQ below says otherwise. Reheat until piping hot throughout.

Questions

Can I prep it the night before?

Prep the yogurt base and berries in a jar, but add granola and chia in the morning or they go soft overnight.

Frozen berries OK?

Yes — microwave 100g (3.5 oz) for 40 seconds and spoon them over warm. The juices marble the yogurt like a sauce.
