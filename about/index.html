How nutrition is calculated

Every figure on this page is calculated from the recipe itself, not estimated: each ingredient above is costed with reference values from USDA FoodData Central (the US Department of Agriculture food database), cooking oils used in the method are included, optional extras are left out, and the batch total is divided by the servings. Calories are rounded to the nearest 5, macros to the nearest gram. You can look up any ingredient in that same database to check the maths. These are calculations from standard reference data, not lab analysis of a cooked dish, and they are not medical advice. Source: USDA FoodData Central.
